## 🚀 Product 1: Polycracker AI Platform ([polycracker.dev](https://polycracker.dev))

Polycracker solves the three biggest headaches in modern AI: vendor lock-in, messy automation diagrams, and models that forget your data.

### 1. ⚡ Universal AI Router
Connect to top-tier models (OpenAI, Anthropic, and more) through a single, dependable connection.

* 🔀 **Automatic Model Failover:** If one provider has an outage, your apps keep running without code changes.
* 📦 **Consistent Output:** Always delivers clean, structured responses formatted exactly how your database or website needs them.
* 💳 **Flat-Fee Metering:** No confusing token math. You pay a clear, flat rate per request using a prepaid wallet—meaning **zero surprise bills** at the end of the month.
* 🧠 **Strict Session Privacy:** Prompts and answers run strictly in temporary memory. Your business queries are **never** stored, sold, or used to train public AI models.

### 2. 🧬 Adaptive Smart Workflows
Most visual automation builders turn into tangled "spaghetti diagrams" the moment you encounter an unexpected edge case. Polycracker replaces messy chains of dozens of nodes with a simple, intelligent 3-step setup.

* 🧠 **Context Injection:** Injects your company guidelines, identity, and rules upfront so downstream steps stay on-brand.
* 💪 **Self-Correcting Execution:** Chooses between strict step-by-step rules or autonomous routing that automatically works around minor data formatting errors without breaking your pipeline.
* 🛡️ **Data Sanitization:** Cleans sensitive customer data, removes private information, and formats everything before it reaches external tools.

### 3. 🛡️ Sovereign Private Memory
Standard AI forgets earlier conversations and hallucinates when reading long documents. Polycracker provides isolated, private company memory vaults.

* 🎯 **Smart Recall:** Instantly finds the exact page or paragraph needed to answer a question accurately without needing to re-read entire manuals.
* 🔒 **Complete Data Isolation:** Upload your company handbooks, support history, and customer guides to a private Canadian server space that is completely cut off from public AI models.
* 🏢 **Flexible Vaults:** Scale from a single project vault for solo creators up to dedicated, air-gapped server environments for agencies and enterprises.

## 📡 Product 2: Polycracker Social Hub ([social-hub.polycracker.dev](https://social-hub.polycracker.dev))

Polycracker Social Hub is a self-hosted, instance-licensed command center built for community managers, developer marketing teams, and client agencies.

* 📖 **Complete Documentation:** [social-hub.polycracker.dev/docs](https://social-hub.polycracker.dev/docs)
* 🎟️ **License Tiers:** Managed centrally through [portal.polycracker.dev](https://portal.polycracker.dev) under **Hub Basic** or **Hub Premium**.

### 🎛️ Key Capabilities
* 🌐 **Post to 20+ Platforms at Once:** Draft once and broadcast simultaneously across X, LinkedIn, Discord, Reddit, GitHub, Bluesky, Mastodon, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and more.
* 🎯 **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Safety:** Inbound mentions and comments are categorized by confidence:
  * 🟢 *Safe & Clear:* Auto-replies with helpful answers (with strict daily limits per user).
  * 🟡 *Needs Review:* Queues the draft in a visual review deck so a human team member can approve, edit, or reject before anything posts.
  * 🔴 *Spam or Low Quality:* Silently dismissed with no reply generated.
* 🛑 **Emergency Kill Switch:** A master safety pause button. If triggered, all outbound posts and auto-replies freeze immediately while inbound alerts continue safely logging.
* 🏢 **Client Workspaces:** Agencies can organize up to 5 separate client brands with strictly isolated accounts, schedules, and assets.
* ⏰ **Business Hours & Quiet Windows:** Automatically holds scheduled posts until approved marketing hours open up in each target timezone.
